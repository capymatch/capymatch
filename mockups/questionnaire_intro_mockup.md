## Content

### Headline
**"Let's Build Your Recruiting Profile"**

### Subheadline
"Answer 7 quick questions so we can match you with the right volleyball programs and coaches."

### Three Benefit Cards

1. **Personalized Matches** (Target icon)
   - "See schools that fit YOUR playing style, academics, and goals"

2. **Save Time** (Clock icon)
   - "Skip programs that aren't a good fit and focus on real opportunities"

3. **Private & Secure** (Lock/Shield icon)
   - "Your information is never shared without your permission"

### CTA Button
**"Get Started →"** (Purple gradient button)

### Footer Note
"⏱️ Takes about 2 minutes"

---

## Why This Works

1. **Builds Trust** - Explains WHY we need the info
2. **Sets Expectations** - "7 questions" and "2 minutes" reduces anxiety
3. **Shows Value** - Users see what they GET in return
4. **Privacy Assurance** - Important for parents concerned about data
